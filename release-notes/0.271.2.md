# 0.271.2 — Focused-header polish

Two small fixes to the focused (drilled-in) header at the top of the list:

- **No more rubber-banding after adding a note.** Right after you added a note,
  the focused header could briefly bounce/scroll on its own — that's fixed, so it
  stays put.
- **The header border tracks its attachment list.** When a focused note's
  attachment list is expanded, the header's bottom border now follows the
  expanded content instead of cutting across it.
