# 0.169.4

A big rework of **Split note** — editing while you split, a live diff, a redesigned
dialog, and the ability to pop the whole thing out into a full tab.

## Edit while you split (cursor mode)

- **The cursor split now acts on the text you see** — if you edit the text box before
  splitting, the split uses your edited version (previously it split the note's
  original text, ignoring edits).
- **See what changed**: when you edit, a read-only **Original** panel and a **Changes**
  panel appear above the editor. Changes is a word-level diff — deletions struck
  through, insertions highlighted — so you can review before committing. The three
  sections (Original / Changes / Your edit) are consistent collapsible framed panels;
  on mobile the review panels start collapsed to save space.

## Redesigned dialog

- **One row of split methods**: `Line`, `Cursor`, `Each line`, `Blank line(s)`,
  `Headings`. Every method now **previews below and confirms with one Split button** —
  the paragraph/line/heading presets no longer fire instantly on click; they show a
  preview of the resulting parts (Part 1, Part 2, …) and split only when you confirm.
- **Bottom action bar**: Cancel (Esc) on the left; a **Nest under original** checkbox +
  the primary **Split** on the right. Nesting (off by default) makes the new part(s)
  children of the original instead of siblings, and applies to every split method.
- **Copyable previews**: the Original and preset-part previews are selectable, each
  with a copy-to-clipboard button.
- Smaller touches: the split-point cursor uses your accent color; **⌘/Ctrl+Enter** (or
  the Split button) confirms in every mode; Tab cycles through the controls and wraps
  around; a disabled preset shows no count.

## Pop out into a tab

- **"Open in a tab"** moves the split into a full pane — great for long notes and for
  mobile, where a dialog is cramped. It continues exactly where the dialog left off
  (your edits, chosen method, nest setting), with roomier previews.
- When you split from the tab, it shows a brief **"Split complete"** with a countdown
  and closes itself, returning you to the tab you came from.
