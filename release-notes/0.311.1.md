# 0.311.1

A round of view + task improvements: a "Launch view" jump list with a header
button, standalone Pinned/Shared views, a Failed-tasks section, a tag filter on
All tasks, richer timeline rows, and the encrypted-pins surfacing completed.

## Launch view

- The view launcher is now **"Launch view"** — a searchable jump-to-any-view
  list where **each view has its own icon**. There's a **Launch view button in
  the note header** (between the actions and link buttons, desktop + mobile), and
  the command is named "Launch view (jump to a Stashpad view)".

## New standalone views

- **Pinned notes** and **Shared** now open as their **own full-tab views** (not
  just sidebar panels) — a step toward letting each panel stand on its own.
- **Notifications** and **Log** are now reachable from the launcher too (the log
  gained its own "Open Stashpad log" command).

## Tasks

- **Failed tasks** get their own **"Failed" section / chip** in the Tasks panel
  and the All-tasks / Daily Review views — a task auto-failed on overdue
  (completed + the `failed` tag) shows there instead of mixed into Completed. The
  timeline surfaces them through its tag filter (`#failed`).
- **Tag filter on All tasks** — a tags dropdown (every tag on any task) that
  combines with the assignment / person / folder / status filters, like the
  timeline already had.
- **Task timeline rows** now show **folder + assignee + due** (overdue in red),
  matching the All-tasks meta line; the meta line's spacing is tighter.

## Encrypted notes

- **Locked pins and locked tasks now surface read-only** across the board: locked
  pinned bundles appear in the Pinned list AND the folder panel; locked tasks
  appear in the Tasks panel, the All-tasks view, and the Daily Review — each under
  a "Locked" heading, click to unlock. This reads only the small plaintext
  placeholder beside a locked note (never its contents) and honors hide-titles.

## Fixes & polish

- The in-view **header title is centered** for every Stashpad view (All tasks,
  Trash, the panels, etc.), not just the note list.
- The **date/time filter bar** no longer shaves the top/bottom of the focus
  outlines on its inputs and buttons.
