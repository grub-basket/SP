# 0.274.0

Three releases' worth of new aggregate views — a vault-wide master index, a task
timeline, a due calendar, and an activity heatmap — plus the groundwork that made
them honest.

## Due calendar (new)

- A new aggregate view: a month grid where each day lists the notes that belong to
  it. A note shows on a day if it was **created** that day, is **due** that day, or
  **links** to that day (`[[2026-08-18]]`) — exactly the same rule as the "filter to
  one day" button, so the calendar and the filter always agree.
- The reason is colour-coded — created, due, and links each have their own icon and
  colour — and each can be toggled off, so you can see just due dates, or just the
  days notes point at.
- Each day shows up to three notes, then a "+N more" that expands a full list for
  that day below the grid. Clicking a note opens it. Obscured notes keep their titles
  blurred here too.
- Month navigation, a "Today" jump, and a folder filter.
- A **calendar button in the ribbon** opens it directly; right-clicking that button
  opens the activity heatmap.

## Activity heatmap (new)

- A GitHub-style calendar of how much you touched your notes each day, read from the
  action log. Darker squares mean busier days.
- Activity is grouped into buckets — created, edited, tasks, attachments, moves &
  renames, encrypt/archive/colour, and deleted. Each bucket can be hidden with one
  click, so the "house-cleaning" activity (moves, encryption chores) can be set aside
  to show just authoring.
- The colour scale is based on the rank of each day's activity, not a raw maximum, so
  a single bulk-import day no longer flattens every ordinary day to the lightest
  shade.
- Range selector for the last 3, 6, or 12 months. Clicking a day lists that day's
  actions — each with the note it touched (which opens on click), the time, and who
  did it.

## All notes — master index (new in 0.273.0)

- A single flat, vault-wide table of every Stashpad note, with facet filters that
  fold most of the long-requested aggregate views into one surface: title, folder,
  author, created/modified dates, and sort.
- Facet filters for author, tag, and colour; toggle chips for notes with attachments,
  imported notes, notes with internal `[[links]]`, notes containing an external
  `http` link, and each folder's home note.
- Search matches across title, folder, tags, and author.
- Obscured notes blur their title in the table and are excluded from search until you
  tap to reveal (a second tap opens).

## Task timeline (new in 0.273.1)

- A Basecamp-style lineup: every task drawn as a horizontal span from its creation to
  its completion on a shared time axis, with month or week ticks. Open tasks run to
  today as a striped bar; completed ones are solid with a check.
- Filters for folder, open/done, and a 30/90-day or all-time window. Clicking a row
  opens the note.
- Completion now records **when** a task was finished (a new `completedAt` stamp),
  written everywhere a task is completed — checkbox, batch toggle, recurrence, task
  triage, and the tasks panel. Tasks completed before this release have no stamp, so
  their end is estimated from the file's last-edit time and the bar says so (a dashed
  edge and a tooltip) rather than presenting a guess as fact.

## Orphans (new in 0.273.1)

- The All-notes index gains an **Orphans** filter: notes whose parent points at an id
  that resolves nowhere in their folder — they exist on disk but no list ever shows
  them. This makes them browsable; repairing them stays with the existing integrity
  check / fix-orphans commands.

## Action log

- In-app edits are now recorded in the action log. Previously only edits made from
  *outside* Stashpad left a trace; edits made through Stashpad's own editor were
  invisible to the log, which is why the activity heatmap needed this to count your
  authoring accurately.

## Under the hood

- The master index, timeline, calendar, and heatmap refresh as notes are created,
  edited, moved, or deleted, and each has a manual refresh button.
