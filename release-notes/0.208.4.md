# 0.208.4

- **Titles no longer show raw Markdown.** A note beginning
  `📕 **Atomic Habits** — James Clear` appeared in the breadcrumb with its
  asterisks intact. Titles are taken from a note's first heading or first line,
  so every syntax character showed through — in the breadcrumb, its tooltips,
  the focused header, the jump-to-level list, the editor, and the Obsidian tab
  title.

  Emphasis, inline code, strikethrough, highlight, links and images, wikilinks
  (showing the alias where there is one), leading heading/quote/bullet markers
  and backslash escapes are now stripped at the single point every title passes
  through. The stripper is deliberately conservative, since the real risk is
  mangling ordinary prose that happens to contain an asterisk or underscore.
