# 0.534.1

This release covers every change since 0.533.1. Pinning a comment to a spot on an image or PDF page in the Showcase is now easy to find. Before, the **Pin a spot** button only appeared after you picked a specific file in the feedback box's dropdown.

## Showcase — pins

- **0.534.0** — Every image and PDF now has a **📍** button in its header, next to ⤢.
  - Click it, then click the spot on the image or page. A short hint says what to do; Esc cancels, and clicking 📍 again cancels too.
  - The comment is aimed at that file automatically.
- **0.534.0** — **Pin a spot** is always shown in the feedback box when the page has an image or PDF. With "Whole section" selected it reads "Click an image or page…", and the image or page you click becomes the comment's target.
- **0.534.0** — While you're placing a pin, that page's 📍 buttons are highlighted.
- **0.534.0** — PDFs shown in the plain viewer (over 60 pages or 60 MB, or files the PDF engine can't open) can't take pins, so they don't show a 📍.
- **0.534.1** — If you start pinning on a PDF that then opens in the plain viewer, pinning cancels itself instead of waiting for a click that can't land.
- **0.534.1** — Clicking 📍 several times shows one hint, not a stack of them.
