# 0.496.0

Drag and drop, and getting text selection out of the way of it.

## Drag onto a note to nest it — the green highlight is back

Dragging a note onto the middle of another note nests it as a child, and the
target row turns green to say so. That green highlight had stopped appearing
for some people, which also made the nest itself unreachable: with no way to
land in the middle band, every drop became a plain reorder.

Two separate faults, both fixed:

- The drop placeholder — the dashed gap that opens to show where the note will
  land — was collapsing to a 4px sliver instead of opening to a full row
  height. It is an empty item in a flex column, so it was the only thing in the
  list that could be squeezed, and it absorbed the whole overflow whenever a
  list's contents already filled the pane.
- The three drop zones (top third inserts above, middle nests, bottom third
  inserts below) were measured against the row's live position. Opening the gap
  above a row pushes that row down, so entering the top third moved the row out
  from under the cursor and the "insert above" zone re-triggered — the row
  outran the cursor and the middle band could never be reached from above. The
  zones are now fixed in place when the cursor enters a row, so the gap can move
  the row without moving the zones.

Because the fault depended on how much spare vertical room the list had rather
than on anything about the notes, it looked random: the same folder would nest
correctly in a short window and refuse to in a tall one.

The drop and the highlight are also now driven by one shared measurement, so a
drop can no longer land differently from what the highlight showed.

## Grab several selected notes from anywhere on the row

With "Select text in notes" on, a note row is not a drag handle — text
selection wins, and dragging is limited to the empty parts of the row such as
the timestamp or the grip. That trade makes sense for one note. It made moving
a group needlessly fiddly: with five notes selected, reordering them still
meant finding a scrap of empty space.

Now, when more than one note is selected, pressing any selected row drags the
whole selection — body text included. The narrower behaviour is unchanged
everywhere else:

- Pressing an unselected row still selects its text.
- Buttons, links, tags, the task checkbox and reactions still work normally on a
  selected row; only text selection yields.
- Holding Alt still selects text, when "Alt+drag selects text" is enabled.

## Text selection yields to an action in progress

Note text stops being selectable while a press on a row clearly means something
else, so a stray highlight can no longer be painted across rows mid-gesture:

- While a group of selected notes is being dragged as a set.
- While a drag is in flight. Any selection made by the press that started the
  drag is also cleared, since it would otherwise survive the drop. Selections
  elsewhere — another pane, another view, the composer — are left alone.
- While the in-list picker is open for "nest under…" or "reply to…", where every
  row is a destination being chosen rather than text to read.
- While a note is sitting on a pending cut or copy, waiting to be pasted.

## Internal

- The reserved zones that are not drag handles are now derived from a single
  list, so the normal set and the multi-selection set cannot drift apart and
  leave a control unclickable.
- Removed a per-row geometry map that the new drop-zone measurement made
  redundant; it was still being rebuilt on every frame of a drag.
- `docs/drag-reserved-zones.md` documents the multi-selection exception.
