# 0.164.6

Small follow-up to the folder-panel button styling from 0.164.5. The three
section-header buttons — **Pinned view options**, **New folder**, and **Open
trash** — keep their accent-colored border, but the icon inside now uses the plain
muted icon color instead of being tinted with the accent. The accent reads as an
outline around the button rather than a color wash over the glyph.
