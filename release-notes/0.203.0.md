# 0.203.0

- **Recurring tasks can repeat on specific days of the week.** The due-date
  dialog gains a row of day chips (S M T W T F S) under the repeat field —
  tap Mon, Wed and Fri and the task repeats on whichever of those comes next,
  keeping its time of day.
- Rules understand the written form too, so `every mon, wed & fri`,
  `every tue/thu` and `every mondays` all work; short and long day names,
  commas, slashes, `&` and `and` are all accepted. Typing a rule lights the
  matching chips, and the chips rewrite the rule — they're two views of the
  same thing.
- Picking days on a rule that wasn't weekly (say `every 3 days`) converts it
  to a weekly one; clearing every chip clears the rule. A "when done" anchor
  survives either way.

---

This release also includes **0.202.1**: pressing ↑ in the edit modal no longer
jumps the cursor to the end of the note (the arrow keys were still reaching the
split view's divider handler, which rebuilt the editor and re-seeded the
cursor). Arrow keys now only move the divider on the split surface.
