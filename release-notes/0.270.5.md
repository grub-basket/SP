# 0.270.5

## The Send button sits above the composer on mobile

On a phone the Send button could be overlapped or miss taps. It shared a row with
the other composer buttons, one of which clips its contents and fades in, so Send
had no layer of its own to sit above. Its tap area also reached close enough to
the right screen edge to fall inside the band where Obsidian listens for the
edge swipe that opens the sidebar, which is the likelier reason taps went astray.

Send is now its own layer, pinned to the bottom-right of the composer and clear
of the edge-swipe band. The layout is unchanged to look at; it just receives its
taps reliably now. Desktop is unaffected.

Selecting text in the composer by dragging still works and still does not open
the sidebar, which was the constraint this had to respect.
