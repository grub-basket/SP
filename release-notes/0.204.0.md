# 0.204.0

- **Recurring tasks can repeat on chosen days of the month.** The due-date
  dialog gains a 1–31 grid (plus **Last**) under the repeat field — pick the
  1st and the 15th and one task covers a twice-monthly chore like checking your
  hours, instead of two separate reminders.
- Written rules work too: `every 1st, 15th of the month`, `every last day`,
  `every 5th & 20th of the month`. Typing lights the matching dates, and the
  grid rewrites the rule — same two-views-of-one-thing behavior as the weekday
  picker.
- A date a month doesn't have is skipped for that month (the 31st happens in
  January but not February), and **Last** always lands on the real last day.
- Weekday and month-day schedules are different kinds of repeat, so picking in
  one clears the other. A "when done" anchor survives either.
