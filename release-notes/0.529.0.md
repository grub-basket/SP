# 0.529.0

This release covers every change since 0.528.1. The ⚡ actions menu in the Stashpad toolbar is much shorter, and you can now customize it.

## ⚡ Actions menu

- **0.529.0** — The ⚡ button in the toolbar acts on the selected notes. On mobile it's the main way to move, delete or copy them. It used to open a single list of about 45 items, so on a phone you had to scroll past Undo, notification history, reload and expand/collapse to reach Move or Delete. The default menu now has 14 rows and fits on screen:
  - Undo · Redo
  - Move to… · Move in list · Delete · Copy ▸ · Edit in Stashpad · Toggle complete
  - Organize ▸ (outdent, reply in-list, color, due date, assign, task, obscure)
  - Open ▸ (new Stashpad tab, Obsidian editor, Launch View, Open Stashpad link)
  - Copy & share ▸ (copy or cut for another vault, Share & export, insert template)
  - Merge & split ▸ (merge selected, merge with, merge into parent, split, fork as a version, mark version as final)
  - View & app ▸ (expand all, collapse all, notification history, reload without saving)
  - More commands…

  Nothing was removed. Every earlier item is either at the top level or inside a submenu. If nothing is selected, items that need a note are greyed out, as before.
- **0.529.0** — There's a new section under **Settings → Note Actions & Menus → "⚡ Actions menu (toolbar)"**:
  - Reorder items by dragging or with the arrows, and remove items you don't use.
  - Add any Stashpad action, or any Obsidian command through **Custom command…**.
  - Add dividers.
  - Create your own submenus, and rename, re-icon and edit the built-in ones. A built-in submenu can be reset to its default, and your own submenus can be deleted.
  - Reset the whole menu to the default.
- **0.529.0** — The **Command icons** list now includes the ⚡ actions and several menu-only actions (Delete, Move in list, View history, Pin to top/bottom of list, Encrypt, More commands). You can change the icon of any built-in ⚡ item. The ⋮ menu's "More commands…" now uses its icon setting too.
- **0.529.0** — The ⚡ menu and the right-click / ⋮ menu are edited separately. Hiding an item in one doesn't hide it in the other.
- **0.529.0** — If you store Stashpad's settings in a vault folder, the ⚡ menu settings are saved in `menus/menus.json` along with the other menu settings, so they sync between devices.
- **0.529.0** — No setup needed. Your existing settings are unchanged, and the new layout applies until you customize it.
