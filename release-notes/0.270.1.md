# 0.270.1

## Focus after a popped-out editor closes

Editing a note in a popped-out tab and then choosing "Open in Obsidian editor"
left focus on the editor tab that had just been spawned, rather than back on the
Stashpad list.

Closing the active tab makes Obsidian move focus to the tab beside it, and the
close was happening before focus was handed back, so the two raced. Focus now
returns to the originating Stashpad tab first, which means the pop-out closes as
a background tab and Obsidian never moves focus at all.

Landing back on the Stashpad tab you came from is unchanged. This makes it
reliable rather than replacing it.

## Pinned notes and filters

Pinned notes can now be kept visible regardless of the filters in effect, not
just the time window, controlled by a new setting under List display. It covers
both kinds of pin, in a list and in the sidebar.

(Superseded the same day by 0.270.2, which replaces the on/off switch with a
three-way choice.)
