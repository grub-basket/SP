# 0.417.0

Mobile layout pass for the Due calendar. It had no mobile-specific rules, so phones inherited the desktop layout: 26px nav buttons and 3px-padded chips/segment (under the 44px tap floor), a wide month label crowding the bar, 84px-tall 7-column cells squeezing note titles and folder names into ellipses, a 10px "+N more", and a Week view of seven side-by-side ~50px lanes.

- **Nav bar → two rows:** `‹ label › Today` on top (the label now stays on one line instead of wrapping), then the Month/Week/Day segment as a full-width, evenly-stretched segmented control (it was previously clipped off the right edge), then the legend chips wrapping beneath.
- **All controls ≥ 40px:** nav buttons 40×40; Today, the segment, and the legend chips padded up to meet the tap-target floor.
- **Month grid:** compact 58px cells; inside a grid cell each note collapses to a colour bar (created / due / link) since a title can't fit in ~50px — tap the day to open the panel with full titles. Scoped to `.stashpad-cal-cell` so the week/day lanes and the day panel keep full rows.
- **"+N more" → "+N"** on mobile (it truncated to "+62 mor"); removed the grey blob Obsidian's mobile button background painted behind it; 24px compact.
- **Week view:** the seven day lanes stack vertically instead of side-by-side.
- Day panel and agenda rows given more room.

Verified at a real 390px viewport (CDP device-metrics override — `emulateMobile` alone doesn't resize the renderer).
