# 0.555.0

This release covers every change since 0.546.2. The biggest part is a design pass on the **Showcase**. It's clearer which picture a reaction or comment belongs to, you can comment right where you click on a picture, each section shows its review decision, and moving between the Showcase and the list keeps your place. Also new: move lines up and down with **Alt+↑/↓** or **Ctrl+↑/↓** in every place you type, faster due times in the due-date picker, and note results in the folder switcher. Fixes include renamed notes going missing from search.

## New

### Move lines up and down

- **0.555.0** — **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓** (Option on a Mac) or **Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓** moves the line the cursor is on up or down one line.
  - Works in the composer, the edit window, edit-in-tab, the detail panel's composer and quick capture.
  - With several lines selected, they move together and stay selected. A selection that ends at the very start of a line doesn't take that line along.
  - **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** undoes a move.
  - On the first or last line nothing happens. In the composer, Alt+↑ on the first line no longer jumps out to the list.
  - On a Mac, Ctrl+↑/↓ is Mission Control by default, so macOS takes it first. Option+↑/↓ always works.

### Due-date picker

- **0.547.0** — Setting a due time with the mouse is quicker.
  - Each quick-adjust chip is now **[− | 15m | +]**, with its own subtract and add halves. The separate +/− switch is gone. Clicking the label adds.
  - Nudges snap to clean times. Minute steps snap to their own grid (9:03 + 15m → 9:15, − 15m → 9:00). Hour steps and longer snap to the whole hour (9:03 + 1h → 10:00).
  - Day and week steps keep the time. A due date with no time stays without one.
  - **Scroll wheel:** over the time box, ±5 minutes (Shift: ±1 hour); over the date box, ±1 day (Shift: ±1 week). Trackpads move about one step per notch.
  - **Arrow keys** in the date and time boxes step the same way (Shift for the bigger step).
  - Scrolling the window down to Repeat no longer changes the date as the pointer passes over a box.
  - **Time chips** set a common time of day in one click. **New setting:** "Due-date time presets" chooses which times show.
- **0.547.1** — The due-date picker looks tidier.
  - One set of text sizes: buttons and boxes share one size, and section headings and hints share a smaller one.
  - **Repeat & reminders** opens from a full-width row with an arrow, a repeat icon and an **Experimental** badge.

### Folder switcher

- **0.553.0** — When nothing matches a folder name, the folder switcher lists matching notes below **+ Create new Stashpad …**.
  - Up to 30 notes from the folders the global search covers, matched with the same search as elsewhere: title matches first, then notes whose text matches, newest first.
  - Text matches show the folder and the matching line.
  - Picking one opens the note in its Stashpad, selected.
  - Notes are only gathered once a search finds no folder, so switching folders stays as fast as before.

### Showcase

- **0.548.0** — Clearer labels and a quieter page.
  - A section's own reactions sit right under its title, labelled **This section**, instead of under the last option, where they looked like that option's.
  - Reactions say what they mean. Section: 👍 Like · 👎 Don't like · ✅ Approve · ❌ Needs changes. Hovering and screen readers give the meaning and who reacted. The exported web page uses the same words.
  - One **Add feedback** button per section instead of a full form under every section. Cancel or Esc closes it and keeps your text; a reply you set aside reopens as a reply.
  - The comment target menu is labelled **About**. Resolve buttons always read **Resolve** / **Resolved**.
  - Section numbers sit in a left margin beside the title. **Open in list** and **Full size** are labelled buttons that shrink to icons on narrow screens.
  - Toolbar: **Redraw** moved to the tab's ⋯ menu. **Stack / Side by side** only shows when a section has 2+ files. Copy link and Export moved into one **Share** menu. **Hide resolved** is a proper on/off toggle.
  - **"N to resolve · Next ↓"** is a button that reaches sections past **Show more**. Otherwise it reads **Nothing left to resolve** or **No feedback yet**.
- **0.549.0** — Going between the list and the Showcase keeps your place.
  - Opening the Showcase from a note with files and nothing under it, or with only comments under it, used to show "Nothing at this level yet". The Showcase now opens a level up, scrolled to that note.
  - Otherwise it opens on the level of the note you're on.
  - Long levels draw about 30 sections at a time, with **Show N earlier** at the top as well as **Show more** at the bottom. Jumping to a far section moves to it instead of drawing everything above it.
  - **Notes under it (N)** on a section opens its own notes as pages.
  - **Open in list** shows the note as a selected, highlighted row among its siblings in the list's front tab. Coming back from the Showcase puts the cursor back on the section you came from.
- **0.550.0** — Comment where you click.
  - Placing a 📍 pin on a picture opens a comment box right at the pin, like design-review tools. Enter posts, Esc closes and keeps the text. The box stays inside the page and follows it as it resizes.
  - Hovering a comment lights up its pins and the option it's about. Hovering a pin lights up its comment.
  - On narrow panes, a 💬 chip in each section title jumps to that section's feedback.
- **0.551.0** — Review progress.
  - Each section shows a decision: **Approved**, **Needs changes** (any ❌ sends it back, and the tooltip says who), or **No decision yet**. With 2+ options it also shows the option with the best score, or a tie.
  - The toolbar shows **"N of M approved · K need changes"**. Clicking it lists every section with its decision, and picking one jumps there.
- **0.554.0** — On an option, ✅ / ❌ read **Select** and **Reject**, and the decision chip says **Selected: Option B**. Section reactions keep Approve / Needs changes.
- **0.554.1** — Back and forward between levels.
  - Levels are history steps: the tab's ← → buttons and mouse back/forward move between them, and land where you were.
  - The breadcrumbs show the way in after the current level: **› Child** for one, or **› N levels ▾** (a menu) for several.
  - Links inside the page open in a new tab. Unsent drafts survive another file opening in the tab.
- **0.554.2** — Pinning is easier to find.
  - Each file card has a **📍 Comment** button, before the full-size button. It's highlighted while you're picking a spot.
  - The closed feedback form has **📍 Comment on a spot** next to **Add feedback**.
  - The 📍 emoji replaces the map-pin icon everywhere pins appear. It isn't read aloud; the button names carry the meaning.
- **0.554.3** — In the list, a comment's file chip opens the Showcase at that comment: it scrolls to the pinned file or PDF page and flashes the comment. Cmd/Ctrl-click still opens the file preview.
- **0.554.4** — Each comment and reply has a menu: the **⋯** button, right-click, or press-and-hold on touch.
  - **Edit** (your own comments), **Copy link**, **Open in list**, and **Delete** (your own comments only, never a page; it says how many notes it removes).
  - Links, text boxes and selected text keep their normal right-click menu.
- **0.554.5** — The file preview window lists the file's 📍 Showcase comments: **"📍 N comments on this file · M to resolve ▾"**. Picking one opens the Showcase at it. **📍 Comment on this** opens the Showcase ready to pin on that file. Shown for pictures and PDFs only.

## Fixes

- **0.547.2** — Renamed notes no longer drop out of search, especially on phones.
  - Editing a note's first line renames its file. The note's saved text was thrown away in that rename, so search matched only a shortened, filename-style title until the row happened to redraw. The saved text now moves with the rename. Renaming a folder also no longer forgets every note's saved text.
  - The note search now runs your query again once note texts finish loading. On a phone, a search typed early used to match titles only.
- **0.553.0** — Notes with an all-number id could not be opened from pinned rows and similar links. They open now.
- **0.546.3** — Turning the debug trace on only says "It's also saved to disk" when **Save the debug trace to disk** is on. Otherwise it says the trace is kept in memory only, so a force-quit loses it, and names the setting to turn on.
- **0.551.1, 0.551.2** — Showcase: notes that are pages (they have files or notes of their own) are no longer counted as feedback.
  - A section of picture pages showed "6 to resolve", could never be done, and offered **Resolve** on its pages. Pages are now left out of every comment and to-resolve count, Next, the sections menu, and the 💬 chip, and get no Resolve button.
  - The reaction bar and the decision chip agree when a ✅ was added from the emoji picker.
  - Focus no longer jumps when the page redraws. **Show N earlier** comes back correctly after switching tabs. Landing on a section works after notes above it were deleted.
- **0.554.6** — Showcase:
  - **📍 Comment on this** works on a PDF that hadn't finished drawing yet. If it can't (hidden section, file not found, PDF shown in the plain viewer), it says why.
  - To-dos typed in the list are no longer counted as sub-pages. The section button reads **Go in (n)**.
  - Set-aside drafts keep their text and stay replies. A reply to a deleted comment becomes plain feedback.
- **0.552.0** — The **Insert at multiple points** panel puts **Insert** and **Cancel** on the left, next to the text box, instead of at the far right edge.
- **0.553.1** — The **Insert at multiple points** text box looks like a text box: a filled background, a border, and an accent outline while you type. It used to blend into the grey strip.

## Internal

- **0.555.0** — `moveLines()` in `src/markdown-input.ts`, a pure function that returns the swapped range, its new text and the new selection. Applied with `execCommand("insertText")` so the browser's undo records it.
- **0.554.4** — `cmdDelete({ targets })` deletes exactly the given notes instead of the list's selection.
- **0.549.0** — `plugin.revealRowByRef(folder, id)` shows a note as a selected row in its level from anywhere. Shared by the Showcase and the comment menu.
- **0.547.2** — `RenderCacheStore.rename(oldPath, newPath)` moves a cached render instead of evicting it.
- The `scripts/obs-test-guard` test helper understands test instances past `z` (`aa` … `zz`).
- Developer-only notes, changelogs and code-map updates (14 commits). They don't ship in the plugin.
