# 0.272.0

## A quick-action menu on every note

Each note now has a star button, next to the usual actions menu, that opens a
short menu of the actions you reach for most. It runs on the note you tapped.

You choose what it contains, in settings (Windows & Tabs, "Quick actions menu"):
tick the actions you want, untick the rest, or clear them all to hide the star
entirely. Out of the box it holds Copy, Move, Blur / unblur, and Reveal in large
text. Also available: copy tree, clone, set colour, set due date, move to
archive, and edit.

## Reveal in large text

A new action that shows a note's text large and high-contrast, filling the
screen, so you can hand your phone or laptop to someone across a table and they
can read it. Plus and minus buttons scale the text and the size is remembered.
Tap the text, press Escape, or hit the close button to dismiss it.
