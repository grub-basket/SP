# 0.271.6

## Attachments stay in the note and appear in the rail

The attachment rail no longer removes anything from a note. An embed or a link
renders inline in the body exactly as it would anywhere in Obsidian, and the
rail is an additional index of the note's files alongside it, so an image shows
both in place and as a chip in the rail. Note references (links to other notes,
dated notes, `.md` links) stay in the body and appear in the backlinks and
outgoing-links rails, as before.

Existing notes pick this up the next time they render.
