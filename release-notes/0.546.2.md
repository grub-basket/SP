# 0.546.2

This release covers every change since 0.545.1. Each note's due date now shows on its footer line, coloured by how soon it's due. A due date with no time (a plain `YYYY-MM-DD`) is now read on the right day. Before, it read as the evening before for anyone west of Greenwich.

## New

- **0.546.0** — The due date shows on the note's footer line.
  - On desktop it comes first, before the edit time: **due Oct 11 9:24 PM · edited …**. On mobile it replaces the edit time.
  - The colour shows how soon it's due:
    - no colour: more than 7 days away
    - yellow: within 7 days
    - orange: within 48 hours
    - red-orange: due today or within 12 hours
    - red: overdue
    - filled red: more than 7 days overdue
  - Completed notes don't show a due date.
  - Setting, adding or removing a due date, or completing the task, updates the footer straight away. The colours refresh every minute.
  - **New setting, under Dates & Time:** the time a due date with no time reminds you on its day. The default is 7:00 AM.

## Fixes

- **0.546.0** — Due dates with no time are read on the right day.
  - A due written as a plain date, like `2026-10-07` (what Obsidian's date property writes), was read as midnight UK time. West of Greenwich that's the evening before.
  - So the reminder came the evening before, the due date window opened on the wrong day, and repeating tasks moved to the wrong evening.
  - Every place that reads a due date now reads it on its own day, in your time zone.
  - A due date with no time now counts as on time for the whole day. Reminders and labels show the date, or **Today**, instead of "12:00 AM".
- **0.546.1** — The **Set due date** window leaves the time box empty for a due date with no time, instead of showing 00:00.
- **0.546.2** — A due date with no time stays that way:
  - **Repeating tasks:** a repeating task moves on to the next date (also when you skip or complete it), not to midnight of that day.
  - **Set button:** pressing **Set** without filling in a time no longer adds 9:00 AM. Typing a phrase without a time, like `friday`, doesn't either.
  - **Tasks panel and timeline:** they show **Today**, and mark the task overdue only once the day is over.
  - **Auto-done delay:** counted from the end of the due day.
  - **Clock-change days:** the end of the day is correct on daylight-saving change days. It used to be an hour off.
- **0.545.2** — Trash view on mobile: the reset button lines up with the mode chips next to it. It used to sit 9px higher.

## Internal

- **0.545.3** — Diagnostic: each background save of Stashpad's settings now records which part of Stashpad made it (for example `"by":"draft-save"`, `"mru"`, `"reaction"`). It appears in the opt-in debug trace (**Diagnostics: turn the debug trace on / off**), which stays local and is never sent anywhere. Nothing else changes. It's there to find the cause of a lag some people see on mobile.
- New `src/due-warmth.ts`:
  - `parseDueString` replaces `Date.parse` everywhere a due is read.
  - `dueDeadlineMs` and `endOfLocalDay` decide when a due actually lapses.
  - `nextDueValue` keeps a repeating plain-date due as a plain date.
  - `reminderAtMs` decides when a plain-date due reminds, and `dueTier` / `dueLabel` / `dueRelative` drive the footer colours and labels.
- Developer changelog notes for the branch. They don't ship in the plugin.
